# SpaceX Falcon 9 Landing Prediction

## Slide 1 — Title
SpaceX Falcon 9 First Stage Landing Prediction

## Slide 2 — Problem statement
- SpaceX reduces cost by reusing Falcon 9 first stages.
- Landing success is a key economic and operational decision metric.
- We aim to predict whether the first stage will land successfully.

## Slide 3 — Data and features
- Flight number
- Payload mass
- Launch site
- Orbit type
- Booster version and reuse indicators
- Landing pad, grid fins, legs, block, reused count

## Slide 4 — EDA insights
- Success rate improves with flight progression.
- Some launch sites show clearer success patterns.
- Payload mass and orbit type influence landing outcomes.

## Slide 5 — Modeling
- Logistic Regression
- SVM
- Decision Tree
- KNN
- Train/test split and model comparisons

## Slide 6 — Results
- Models provide meaningful predictive performance.
- Best model selected based on testing accuracy and confusion matrix.
- The system is suitable for data-driven launch analysis.

## Slide 7 — Conclusion
- The project demonstrates a reliable predictive workflow.
- It is strong for portfolio, research, and business presentation use.
